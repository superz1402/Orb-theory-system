# Monetization & Policy Guardrails (YouTube)

Distilled 2026-10-25 from YouTube's channel monetization policies as researched
for this project. **Re-check the live policy pages before launch and quarterly**
— this file is a guardrail summary, not legal advice and not a substitute for
the current policy text.

## The core rule: "inauthentic content"
- YouTube does **not** ban faceless channels or AI-assisted production.
- On **15 July 2025** YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" policy to
  **"inauthentic content"**: content that is **mass-produced, generic, repetitive,
  or manipulative** is ineligible for monetization. The review applies to the
  **channel as a whole** — one batch of templated uploads can endanger everything.
- What fails: interchangeable videos (same template, nouns swapped), image
  slideshows / scrolling text with little narrative value, readings of material
  the creator didn't write, repurposed footage without significant original
  commentary — **even with the original creator's permission**.
- What passes: an original script with a specific angle per video; a recurring
  series format/characters where each episode has a distinct storyline; AI used
  to *enhance* storytelling (script help, a unique visual identity), with the
  creator's own research, framing, and decisions visible in the result.

## How Orb Theory complies (by design)
1. Original, per-episode research dossiers and scripts (facts gate).
2. Real, case-specific archival imagery with transformation through narration,
   edit, timeline graphics, and grade — not reused clips with a voiceover pasted on.
3. A consistent but non-templated identity: same narrator/format, different
   story, visuals, and structure per episode.
4. Quality over volume: long-form first (proposed 1 long-form episode/week,
   pending Bilal's approval — 2026-10-26 pivot), each passing all gates.

## Reused content
- Never upload others' videos/clips/compilations as content. Brief, credited
  still images under PD/CC licenses, transformed by original narration and edit,
  are this channel's model — keep the credits ledger immaculate (license gate).

## Synthetic / altered content disclosure
- On every upload, set YouTube's **altered/synthetic content** disclosure for the
  AI narration (and any realistic AI imagery). The disclosure is a label for
  viewers, not a monetization penalty; **failing** to disclose risks removal /
  Partner Program action.
- The metadata template includes a written disclosure line for the description.

## YPP thresholds (targets, verify at launch)
- 1,000 subscribers **and** 4,000 valid public watch-hours in 12 months, **or**
  1,000 subscribers and 10M valid Shorts views in 90 days. AdSense linking,
  identity, tax, and bank details are **account-holder-only** steps (Bilal).

## Content risks to avoid entirely
- YMYL presented as expertise: AI personas posing as medical/legal/financial
  experts. Orb Theory explains history/science as curiosity — never advice.
- Shock/distress formulas, emotionally manipulative templates, stitched
  unrelated AI clips ("unsatisfying content" enforcement).
- Copyright: any asset without a recorded free license (see license gate).
